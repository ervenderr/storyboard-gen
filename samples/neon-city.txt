A rainy night in a neon-lit megacity: tall glass towers, glowing signs in pink and cyan, and wet streets reflecting the lights. In a narrow alley, steam rises from vents beside a noodle stall with a flickering sign. On the rooftop of a skyscraper, a helipad overlooks the whole glittering skyline through the clouds. Inside an underground train station, empty platforms stretch under cold fluorescent lights. Before sunrise, the city quiets down and a calm harbor with distant cranes glows in soft orange and teal light.
