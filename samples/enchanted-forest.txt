A traveler enters an ancient forest at dawn, where tall mossy trees filter pale golden light onto a winding path. Deeper in, the path crosses a stone bridge over a glowing blue stream surrounded by giant mushrooms. At midday the trail climbs to a hilltop clearing with a crumbling watchtower overlooking endless green valleys. By dusk, the forest thins out to a quiet lake with floating lanterns drifting across the water under a purple sky. At night, a cozy wooden cabin glows with warm light among the pines, with fireflies in the air and stars above.
